Before you call

Straight answers.

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

There's an after-hours call-out fee, and we quote it to you on the phone before we get in the van. It's rolled into the flat price for the job — never added afterwards as a surprise line item.

Will I get a price before the work starts?

Always. We diagnose first, then give you one flat number covering labour, parts and cleanup. If you'd rather not proceed, you pay the diagnostic fee and we leave the site tidy.

What if the fix needs a part you don't carry?

The vans stock the common failures — valves, flex lines, cartridges, wax rings, water heater elements. For anything else we make the system safe, get you a firm return time, and don't charge a second call-out.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, for water heater replacements, repipes, gas work and anything structural. Permit and inspection costs appear in the quote you approve, and we handle the scheduling with the inspector.

Are you licensed and insured?

Licensed, bonded and fully insured, with general liability and workers' compensation cover. Ask for the certificate and we'll email it before we arrive.

How do I shut the water off right now?

Look for the main shutoff where the supply enters the building — usually a lever or gate valve near the meter, water heater or front hose bib. Turn it fully clockwise. Can't find it? Call us and we'll walk you through it.
